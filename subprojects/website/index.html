What works today

  • Output over Art-Net, sACN and USB (ENTTEC Pro protocol) at 40 Hz, with outputs you can change while it runs.
  • Patch with a fixture library, a profile editor and GDTF import.
  • Programmer with a Titan-style command line, wheels, fan and multi-cell fixtures.
  • Colour as one value per fixture: HSI, RGB, CMY or a picker, plus extra emitters.
  • Palettes, groups, cues, cue lists and chases on playbacks.
  • MIDI and OSC control, including the Behringer BCF2000 and TouchOSC.
  • Show files with undo and autosave; output keeps running if the app hangs.

Install

  1. Open the DMG, drag CMDMX onto Applications, then eject the DMG.
  2. The app is not notarised, so macOS blocks the first open:
    • macOS 14: right-click the app in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.
    • macOS 15 or later: double-click the app once and close the warning. Then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. After that the app opens normally, until you install the next version.

On first use macOS may ask for Local Network access (allow; needed for Art-Net and sACN) and for incoming connections (allow; needed for OSC and TouchOSC).
