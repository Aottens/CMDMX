## Findings (owner, during the run)
1. F1–F4 work, but only with fn held (macOS puts its own functions on the F-keys by default). Consider a second shortcut (e.g. ⌘1–⌘4) with the Keys work in UX-02c.
2. With a sub-selection (Odd/Even) it is hard to see which fixtures are targets: the double blue outline differs too little from the plain selected outline.
3. Patch screen: after clearing the search field the old results stay listed; an empty search should empty the list.
4. Dragging a fader up, it lags behind the pointer and bounces: the engine's older deltas overwrote the dragged value. Fixed with touch behaviour (UI-22 amended).
