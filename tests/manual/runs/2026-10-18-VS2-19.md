## Findings
- Master time 2 s + overlap 50 % on Art-Net: 201 red from 31.13 s to 33.12 s, 208 (eighth) from 38.14 s to 40.12 s: one second apart, 2 s each, about 9 s in all. ("Blue" from the picker has R = 2, hence the 2 at rest.)
- Checked on Art-Net (no lamps at home): 201 faded red → blue from 20.05 s to 23.04 s, linear, one step per 25 ms tick.
- Palette cells feel small next to a real Titan (owner); the icons are hard to read at this size. UI-05 fixes 54 pt cells in 8 columns — revisit the cell size / columns per window.
- The softkeys header truncates "SOFTKEYS · RECOR…" (owner): the context name must fit (shorter label or smaller tracking).
- Mask softkeys: no visible difference between a group that is in the mask and one that is not (owner). Needs a clear on/off state (Titan: inverted softkey).
- Owner: a running fade is invisible in the app. Decision (a): the Programmer window keeps the target value in cyan and marks fading values amber with their progress until done (amend PAL-26/30, this task); the live output itself comes with IDEA-10 (lamps on fixture buttons).
- After Master Time / Master Overlap the softkeys leave the Palette context (the line is cleared, and the context follows the line), so the new value is not visible (owner). The Palette softkeys should stay open after setting a value, until Cancel or Exit, as Titan's Palette menu does.
