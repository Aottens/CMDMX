# App cue lists run — VS3-05 (2026-10-21)

No Swift test target exists (CLAUDE.md), so the Go block, Cue list window, Cue view, strips,
Record softkeys, Playback Options tabs and Space of `specs/modules/cuelists.md` CUE-42..47 and 54
(and `ui.md` UI-03, 13, 18, 19, 22, 23 as amended for VS3-05) are checked by hand. Copy to
`tests/manual/runs/<date>-<task>.md` and tick it there. This run is VS3-01's demo (CUE-48).

Setup: two terminals, and a fresh shows folder so your real one stays untouched.
- `rm -rf /tmp/cmdmx-cue`
- Daemon: `cd subprojects/engine && cargo run -p cmdmx-daemon -- --no-sacn --show-dir /tmp/cmdmx-cue`.
- App: `cd subprojects/app && swift build && scripts/bundle-app.sh && open .build/ColourmotionDMX.app`.
- Demo show: church rig (washes 101–104, pars 201–226, front spots 301–306); pages 1 and 2 hold
  the demo playbacks, page 3 is empty. Workspace 4 (Playbacks) shows the Cue list window.

Output over time cannot be seen without lamps: Claude listens to the daemon's Art-Net on
universe 1 during the run and reports the measured values and times.

## Findings and fixes during the run
Fixed in the PR (re-checked in the app):
- Cancel on the Record softkeys left the amber prompt; every Cancel clears it, and the used-handle
  prompt names Append and Insert.
- Time fields and the BPM field showed the old value until typed twice; they show what was sent.
- Playback Options Exit was small in the title row: a full-size blue button by the tabs. Its
  "Options: tap a playback handle" prompt is cleared when the screen opens.
- Strip legend: the meta line ("BPM 1 · 120 ×1", "2/3 · 128") was cut off next to the icon; it
  now takes the full width. The value icon reads "DIM / 45%" instead of "DIMME…".
- With a list and a chase running the app sat at 100 % CPU and showed stored edits seconds late
  (or not until an input event): live deltas now redraw at most every 1/30 s (~50 % CPU; the
  remaining cost is one ObservableObject for every zone, marked ponytail).

Open points (not fixed here):
- A BPM master with fader Multiplier shows its stored multiplier (×1) on the strip while the
  fader picks ×2; the tempo shown is the effective one.
- The Cue list tab shows tracking options on a chase, and the Fader tab is shown on cue lists
  where fader modes do not apply (CUE-15).
- The group-times row shows "—" buttons with an empty fade row until a group is given times.
- Driving the app by computer use: "@" typed as a keystroke sometimes arrived as Group; the keypad
  click works. Not seen with a real keyboard.
