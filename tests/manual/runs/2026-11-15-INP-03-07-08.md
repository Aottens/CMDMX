## Found in the run
- At launch SwiftUI gave the keyboard to the Find field of the Fixtures window in a hidden workspace, so ⌥R typed "®"
  into it and Keys typed nothing on the line. That is also why computer use "could not send keystrokes to the app" in
  the CTR-06 run. A field focused before a workspace switch kept the keyboard the same way. Fixed in this PR: hidden
  workspaces are disabled, so their fields can neither take nor keep focus.
- The Console menu, in `CMDMXApp`'s commands, was rebuilt on every engine change while the App observed the engine; the
  App now holds it without observing (UI-70).
- `scripts/app-cpu.sh`: idle 2.1 %, demo 10.8 % (again: idle 2.3 %, demo 12.5 %). On this Mac `main` gave idle 2.9 and
  4.5 % in two runs; the first try of this build, before the App change, 5.5 %.
