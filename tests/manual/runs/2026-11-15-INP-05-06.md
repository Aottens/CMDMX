## Found in the run
- The Move message did not show at first: it was said while Move was still armed, so it counted as Move's own line and
  went when Move disarmed 250 ms later, and the move's delta could also come before the `usage` answer. Fixed in this
  PR: the line is not the function's own, and it is tried on both the delta and the answer.
- `scripts/app-cpu.sh`: `app-cpu: idle 3.6 %, demo 13.2 %` (budgets 5 % and 15 %). Two earlier runs gave idle 5.4 and
  5.9 %; side by side with `main`'s app both builds gave 2.2–3.9 %, so that was the machine, not this build.
