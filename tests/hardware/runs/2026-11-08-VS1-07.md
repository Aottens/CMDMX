## What the BCF sends in MC mode (CTL-33)
Measured on the raw MIDI port.
- Upper button row: notes 16..23. These are Mackie's MUTE notes, not REC. Lower row: notes 24..31 (SELECT)
- LEDs: note 16..23 lights the upper button of that channel, 24..31 the lower. Notes 0..3 (REC) light nothing
- Four right-hand buttons: 1 Rewind (91), 2 Forward (92), 3 Stop (93), 4 Play (94)
- Encoder-group buttons: pressed left to right, two sent notes, 41 and then 40; the other two sent nothing
- Bank: notes 46 and 47. Channel (48, 49): never seen

## Found and fixed in this run
- CTL-33 did not fit the BCF: the upper row sends MUTE notes, so it did nothing. Notes 16..23 now also bind to `upper`.
  The profile went from 41 to 49 bindings
- CTL-17 was not met: an `upper` or `lower` LED looked at Flash only, so a Swop lit nothing and a Flash lit both
  LEDs. An LED now shows its own button's function
- `tests/hardware/bcf2000.md` named the wrong power-on button and had Flash and Swop the wrong way round

## Resilience
- [ ] Power-cycle: over DIN the daemon cannot see this. No warning, no crash, input works at once, but motors and LEDs
      stay wrong until the device is unticked and ticked in Control, then at once (CTL-16, 21); see Open points

## Open points
- On this Mac the BCF's USB output does not work: CoreMIDI reports success, nothing arrives, and libusb's bulk OUT on endpoint 0x02 fails with `0xe0004061`. The endpoint declares a packet size of 4, likely the cause. A driver cannot fix this, so the checklist now uses DIN → `tests/hardware/bcf2000.md`
- The BCF2000 has no touch sensor (the touch note only comes with movement, off about 0.1 s after stopping), so CTL-20's hold cannot apply to it → `tests/hardware/bcf2000.md`
- The BCF2000 has no Channel buttons, so slots 9 and up of a larger page cannot be reached from it → IDEA-48
- Over DIN a power-cycled BCF gets no fresh feedback until it is re-enabled; it does send its Mackie sysex at power-on, which could trigger that → IDEA-48
- A surface behind a MIDI interface does not match the profile by name, so the mapping is chosen by hand → IDEA-48
