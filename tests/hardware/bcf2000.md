# BCF2000 checklist — Mackie Control profile (VS1-07, CTL-01..04, 17, 20, 21, 30..33)

Gate 3: only the owner ticks these. Copy to `tests/hardware/runs/<date>-VS1-07.md` for a run.

Setup: BCF2000 in **Mackie Control mode**: hold the **second button from the left in the top row** while powering on,
until the display shows `NC C` (holding too long gives `EG`). **Over DIN MIDI on Apple Silicon**: the BCF's USB output
does not work on the owner's M4 Mac (2026-11-08 run). A USB-MIDI interface both ways, BCF USB unplugged, and global
setup `S-4` (`EDIT`+`STORE`, encoder 1, `EXIT`; `U-1` has DIN off). Pick the mapping by hand: the profile matches by
name and sees the interface's. Daemon running, demo show (`faders_per_page` 8, playbacks 1–8 on page 1). Output visible
on Art-Net (Claude listens) or in the app.

## Attach
- [ ] macOS Audio MIDI Setup shows the port, in and out. Name as the daemon lists it: ________
- [ ] The device appears `Disabled`; `control.enable` (or the Control screen) enables it and it takes
      the mapping **Mackie Control** (`profile: true`) (CTL-24, 30)
- [ ] On enable the motor faders move to the levels of page 1 (all at 0 in the demo show) (CTL-21)

## Faders and pages
- [ ] Fader 1 up → playback 1 rises in the app and on DMX; full travel reads 0 % … 100 % exactly (CTL-01, 31)
- [ ] Faders 2–8 move playbacks 2–8 (the BCF2000 has no master fader; a surface that has one moves the GM) (CTL-33)
- [ ] Bank ▶ → page 2: the motor faders move to page 2's levels within ~0.3 s; Bank ◀ → back to
      page 1 and the motors return (CTL-21, 32)
- [ ] A page change on the screen moves the motors too (CTL-21)
- [ ] Surfaces with touch sensors only (the BCF2000 has none): touch a fader without moving it: no level jump; hold it
      while the level changes on the screen: the motor does not fight the hand; at release it snaps to the engine
      level (CTL-20)
- [ ] Surfaces with Channel buttons only (the BCF2000 has none): set `faders_per_page` 10 in a show: Channel ▶ puts
      slots 9–10 on faders 1–2 and faders 3–8 go to 0 and do nothing; Channel ◀ goes back (CTL-31, 32)

## Buttons and encoders
The handle buttons do what `handle_buttons` assigns (CUE-25). On a memory the upper is Swop and the lower Flash. To
tell them apart, keep another playback up: Swop darkens it while held, Flash leaves it.
- [ ] Upper row of channel 1 → playback 1's upper button; only its own LED follows (CTL-17, 33)
- [ ] Lower row of channel 1 → playback 1's lower button; only its own LED follows
- [ ] V-Pot 1 turned with a fixture selected → wheel 1 moves (positive = clockwise); its ring shows
      the value as one dot (CTL-04, 33)
- [ ] V-Pot 1 push → Fix ▶ (`key step_next`)
- [ ] Play → Go, Rewind → Back on the connected cue list; Stop holds a running fade (give a cue a fade time first,
      CUE-12: with nothing running Stop does nothing)

## What the BCF sends in MC mode (CTL-33, measured 2026-11-08)
- Upper row 16..23 (Mackie's MUTE notes), lower row 24..31 (SELECT); the same notes light those LEDs. REC notes light nothing
- Four right-hand buttons: 91 Rewind, 92 Forward, 93 Stop, 94 Play
- Encoder-group buttons: two of the four send notes, 41 and 40. Bank 46 and 47

## Resilience
- [ ] Power-cycle the BCF while the daemon runs: no crash, input works at once. Over USB also one `BCF2000 disconnected`
      warning, re-attached within 2 s and the motors back to the current levels. Over DIN the daemon cannot see this;
      unticking and ticking the device in Control sends everything again (IDEA-48) (CTL-16, 21)
